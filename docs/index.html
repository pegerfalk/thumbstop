Thumbstop

Stop the scroll.

An agent skill that turns the project you're working on into a short vertical video for TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels. One command: /thumbstop. It reads your project, finds the one idea worth saying, and builds the video from your own words and visuals.

The name comes from the thumb-stop: the moment a scrolling thumb stops on a video. Thumbstop is designed to stop your thumb.

Install

Claude Code
> /plugin marketplace add pegerfalk/thumbstop
> /plugin install thumbstop@thumbstop
Cursor, Codex, Copilot, Gemini CLI, opencode and more
$ npx skills add https://github.com/pegerfalk/thumbstop --skill thumbstop
Then, in any project
> /thumbstop
> /thumbstop --voice

What it does

  • Hook first. Three hooks, scored; the first second carries an image, the hook line and a sound.
  • True claims only. Every number and claim traces to a file in your project, or it is cut.
  • Never a still frame. Lines build phrase by phrase on the music's beat; something moves about every second beat.
  • Platform-safe. All text stays out of the TikTok, Instagram and Facebook UI zones, checked automatically.
  • Real product on screen. Your own media when you have it, otherwise the product rebuilt from its source.
  • Ready to post. An export without music (plus one with, when you add a track), a cover baked in as frame 0, and a post text per platform.

Launching or posting?

/brag

A landscape launch video of the thing you built. For the launch post.

/thumbstop

Vertical videos for the feed, made to stop the scroll. For every post after.
