# Music licence

Thumbstop recommends five tracks for its `-music` export but does **not**
ship the audio files: download each one from its song page (below) and keep
the file name. Only the cue presets in `cues/` ship with the skill. The tracks
were first bundled by `/brag` 0.3.0 without author or licence metadata;
provenance and licence below were verified on 2026-09-23.

## Licence

**CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with the
author's waiver making attribution optional. Standard licence page:
https://ende.app/en/standard-license. Verbatim, retrieved 2026-09-23:

- "Actually quite simple: CC BY 4.0 – but with an upgrade from me."
- "Commercially? Yes, absolutely!"
- "You do NOT have to credit me."
- FAQ (https://ende.app/en/faq): the licence covers "…podcasts, commercials,
  indie games, YouTube, TikTok…"; "Rule of thumb: if it's not expressly
  forbidden there, you're allowed to do it."
- Condition: "The music must be part of your work (background, intro, etc.)."

Both pages were read on 2026-09-23. Re-read them before relying on this
summary; the author's terms are the source of record.

## No-gos (the author's)

- "Never register my music with YouTube Content ID or similar systems." This
  also covers Meta Rights Manager and TikTok's equivalent.
- Do not upload the music unchanged as your own song (Spotify, Apple Music…).
- Do not distribute the files standalone (which is why this skill does not
  ship them).

## If a claim arrives

A third party may wrongly register a track. Dispute the claim, cite
https://ende.app/en/standard-license and the track's song page, and re-post
the `-nomusic` export if needed.
